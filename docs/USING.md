# Using your reading library

## Getting in

- Open your site in **Safari** and log in with your password.
- You stay logged in for about 6 months on that phone.
- **Add it to your Home Screen:** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The "Aa" icon then opens straight into your library.

## Adding a PDF

1. Tap **Choose File**. Pick a PDF from **Files**, **iCloud Drive**, or wherever it's saved. Email attachments can be saved to Files first.
2. Pick a **Converter**:
   - **Fast**: seconds. Right for most documents, including two-column papers.
   - **Careful (Docling)**: about a minute. Use it when Fast gets the order of the text or a table wrong.
   - **Careful + equations (slow)**: several minutes. Only for papers with a lot of maths.
3. Tap **Convert**.

You can leave the page while it converts. When you come back, the document is at the top of the list. One PDF is converted at a time, so if you add several, they wait in a queue.

## Reading

Tap a document to open it.

- **Aa** button: text size (A− / A+), side margin, font, colours and spacing. Your choices are remembered for every document.
- **☰** button: contents. Tap a heading to jump there; the section you're in is shown in bold.
- The orange bar at the top shows how far through you are.
- **‹ Library** at the top goes back.
- Your place in each document is remembered, and the list shows how much of each you've read.

## The ⋯ menu next to each document

- **Convert again**: choose a different converter and redo it. Your place in the list stays the same.
- **Original PDF**: download the PDF you uploaded.
- **Delete**: removes the document and its PDF from the server.

## If something looks wrong

- **Text in the wrong order, or a mangled table:** use **Convert again** with **Careful (Docling)**.
- **A word or heading shows twice, or a page number is left in the text:** that's the converter. Try the other one.
- **Scanned PDFs** (photos of paper): if the result is empty or only shows pictures, use **Careful (Docling)**, which reads text out of images.
